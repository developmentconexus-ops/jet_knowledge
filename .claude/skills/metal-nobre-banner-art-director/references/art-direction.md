# Metal Nobre Art Direction

## Core Aesthetic

Architectural editorial + premium product curation.

Think:
- contemporary architecture;
- refined materials;
- quiet luxury;
- believable light;
- tactile surfaces;
- controlled color;
- strong product storytelling.

Avoid:
- marketplace clutter;
- promotional stickers;
- generic luxury clichés;
- too much gold;
- fake UI;
- over-decoration;
- visual noise.

## Brand Consistency

Consistency comes from:
- quality;
- restraint;
- hierarchy;
- typography;
- product fidelity;
- material realism.

Consistency does **not** mean:
- same room;
- same camera angle;
- same palette;
- same composition;
- same products.

## Product-First Thinking

The environment should support the products.

If removing the logo makes the banner look like it could belong to any random premium brand, the product story is probably too generic.

Use real product references whenever fidelity matters.
